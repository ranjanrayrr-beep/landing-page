plants mainly grow in the forest area. Those plants grow under the shades of big trees do not get proper sunlight, they adjust with the less amount of light ,they are called shade plants/indoor plants/low light plants. Indoor plants purify the air in the house.

General Care

  1. keep it in such a place where the morning sun shines and the rest of the times bright light of the day falls.
  2. indoor plants can be placed under the shed of the roof or inside the room.
  3. water the plants twice a month. Before watering check the soil with hands to see if water is needed or not.
  4. Due to storage of water in soil-- leaves turn yellow/tips turn brown or black or plants droop/ leaves shrink.
  5. If soil dries up water to be added otherwise leaves will shrink.
  6. use npk 19:19:19/ npk 20:20:20, 1-2 months interval, mixing 5gm. with per litre of water in the soil / spray on the leaves.
  7. Add a handful of vermicompost every 2-3 months; don't use too much chemical fertiliser.
  8. Don't use mustard cake, it will cause root rot.
  9. New plant should be planted in prepared loose soil , not in compact soil, compact soil must be washed away by water without damaging the roots.
  10. Soil preparation:-- 30% soil + 30% cocopeat/saw dust + 20% vermicompost + 20% sand; perlite/ vermiculite may be mixed(optional), Trichoderma(fungicide) must be mixed in small amount during soil prepn.
  11. Drainage system of the pot should be proper so that water passes out easily.
  12. At first put the plant in a earthen pot for some days to grow it well, then put the earthen pot in a ceramic pot(optional).
  13. Wipe the top and bottom of the leaves with wet cotton cloth at least once a week.
  14. water to be sprayed on the leaves 2-3 times a week.
  15. soil has to be loosen from time to time without damaging root.
  16. Dead branches ,dried leaves or burnt leaves be removed.
  17. Don't expose indoor plants in scorching sunlight.
  18. Don't keep it in AC room
  19. In rainy season soak plants in rain water 2-3 times.
  20. If plants droop due to lack of oxygen in compact soil then add 6% hydrogen peroxide (5 ml. in half liter of water ).
  21. Don't keep the plants below the temperature 15 degree celcius
  22. If flower blooms remove it to extend the life time of the plant.
  23. Always maintain the moisture of the soil.
